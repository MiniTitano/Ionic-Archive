How buying works

Can I buy directly on this site?

No. This site is the archive: every piece is photographed, tested and described here so you can take a proper look first. When you're ready, the Buy on eBay or Buy on Vinted button on the piece's page takes you to its listing, and you check out there.

Why eBay and Vinted?

You pay through a platform you already know, with its own checkout and buyer protection. We never see your card details, and you don't need an account with us.

How do I know the listing is the same camera?

Every listing title carries the piece's archive code (for example IA-0044) and uses the same photos you see here. If anything doesn't match, message us before buying.

The price on eBay is different from the one here. Why?

Platform fees and shipping options can change the final total a little. The listing on eBay or Vinted always shows the price you'll actually pay.

What does "Reserved" mean?

Someone has bought or made an offer on the piece and the sale is being completed on eBay or Vinted. If it falls through, the piece comes back to the archive. You can ask to be notified.

Can I make an offer or ask a question?

Yes: use the platform's message or offer button on the listing, or write to us directly (details below).

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

We ship within 24–48 business hours of the sale, tracked. Cost and carrier are shown on the eBay or Vinted listing, and tracking appears in your order on the platform.

How are the cameras packed?

Double box, custom-cut foam, tapes and batteries packed separately from the body. Your Dropout minifigure travels in the same parcel.

Returns & warranty

Can I return a piece?

Returns follow the platform you bought on. On eBay, the return policy is stated on each listing and you're covered by the eBay Money Back Guarantee. On Vinted, you're covered by Vinted's Buyer Protection: report any problem through the app within the time it gives you after delivery.

Minifigures

Can I choose which figure I get?

No: they're blind boxes. That's the fun. The odds are listed on the Dropouts page.

What colour will my figure be?

A surprise. Every Dropout is 3D printed in a random filament colour, so the colours you see on the site are only examples. Yours could be any of them, or one we haven't shown.

What is the Glow chase?

About 1 bag in 20 hides a glow-in-the-dark edition. It can be any figure, from the most common to the secret one: same pose, printed in filament that lights up in the dark.

Can I buy them separately?

No. Figures are never sold: you only get one by buying a piece from the archive. One per parcel, however many pieces it contains.
